# TASK-08 — Android Home Screen Widgets & Shortcuts

## Objective
Make the most frequent finance actions available without navigating through the full app.

## Requirements
Create configurable Android widgets for:
- current balance
- monthly spending
- budget remaining
- upcoming bills
- quick transaction entry

Add app shortcuts for:
- Add expense
- Add income
- Add transfer
- Open review inbox

## Privacy
Provide a privacy mode that hides exact balances/amounts on widgets. Respect app lock settings and avoid exposing sensitive details on the lock screen where inappropriate.

## Engineering
Keep widgets synchronized efficiently without excessive background work or battery usage.
