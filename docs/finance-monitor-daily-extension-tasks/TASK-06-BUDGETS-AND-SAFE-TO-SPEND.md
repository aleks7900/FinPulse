# TASK-06 — Practical Budgets & Safe-to-Spend

## Objective
Turn budgets into an everyday decision tool.

## Requirements
- Monthly overall and per-category budgets.
- Show spent, remaining and projected amount.
- Calculate remaining days in the budget period.
- Add a deterministic "Safe to Spend" figure based on available discretionary money, known upcoming obligations and configured savings targets.
- Clearly explain which amounts are included in the calculation.
- Add threshold alerts.
- Support rollover as an optional budget setting.
- Handle income/expense refunds correctly.
- Exclude transfers from spending unless explicitly modeled otherwise.
- Add daily/weekly pacing indicators.

## Safety
Do not present Safe to Spend as guaranteed future availability or professional financial advice. Make assumptions visible.

## Testing
Cover zero income, overspent budgets, future bills, refunds, transfers, rollover and multi-currency behavior.
