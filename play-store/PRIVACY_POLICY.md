## 1. Introduction & Privacy Philosophy

FinPulse ("we", "us", "our", or "the App") is an Android personal finance tracking application engineered with an **offline-first and privacy-first architecture**. 

Our core privacy principles are:
- **Offline By Default:** You can use FinPulse completely anonymously without creating an account, without signing in, and without an active internet connection.
- **Zero Advertising & Zero Data Selling:** We do not display advertisements, do not include third-party advertising SDKs, do not collect Google Advertising ID (AAID), and never monetize, sell, or rent your personal or financial data to data brokers or third parties.
- **No Third-Party Analytics Tracking:** FinPulse does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Facebook Pixel, or Mixpanel) that monitor user behavior.
- **User Ownership & Total Control:** Your financial data belongs strictly to you. Cloud synchronization is entirely optional. When activated, data is strictly isolated to your authenticated account with end-to-end user deletion options.

## 2. Information Handled by FinPulse

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device (Offline)
When using FinPulse, the following user-created information is stored exclusively within your device's private, sandboxed SQLite database (Android Room) located in `/data/data/md.alexlab.finpulse/`:
- **Financial Transactions:** Transaction amounts, timestamps, types (Income, Expense, Transfer, Refund), descriptions, merchants, custom tags, and notes.
- **Financial Accounts & Portfolios:** Account names, types (Cash, Checking, Savings, Credit Card, Investment, Loan, Digital Wallet), initial balances, currency codes, and credit limits.
- **Budgeting Configuration:** Category budget targets, overall monthly budgets, rollover configurations, and pacing preferences.
- **Recurring Commitments & Subscriptions:** Billing frequencies, next due dates, merchant names, expected costs, and pause/active statuses.
- **Financial Goals & Debt Payoff Plans:** Target savings dates, milestone targets, debt balances, interest rates, minimum monthly payments, and simulated payment strategies (Snowball/Avalanche).
- **Imported Financial Statements (CSV):** Bank statements imported locally for reconciliation. Statement contents and parsing are processed 100% on-device and never transmitted to remote servers.
- **Application Preferences & Security Flags:** Selected base currency, UI language preference, dark/light theme, balance privacy masking toggle, screenshot protection toggle (`FLAG_SECURE`), and salt/hash credentials for local PIN verification.

### B. Information Processed via Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Sync
If you explicitly choose to activate cloud synchronization by signing into your Google Account via Android Credential Manager, the following data is transmitted and processed:
- **Account Identifier:** Pseudonymous Firebase User ID (`uid`) generated by Firebase Authentication.
- **Profile Metadata:** Google display name, email address, and profile photo URL retrieved from your Google Account to populate your active profile view within settings.
- **Synchronized Application Settings:** Application configuration preferences (base currency code, UI language, dark/light theme mode, balance privacy masking toggle, widget privacy mode, app lock timeout, backup reminder interval, and financial digest schedule preferences) stored under `/users/{userId}/settings/app` to automatically restore your preferences when installing FinPulse on other devices.
- **Synchronized Financial Ledgers:** An encrypted cloud copy of your accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring bills, goals, debts, and import profiles transmitted to Google Cloud Firestore under your isolated user path: `/users/{userId}/`.
- **Sync Timestamps:** Monotonically increasing timestamps used deterministically for last-write-wins conflict resolution across multi-device synchronizations.

**Explicit Local-Only Security Secrets Partition:**
FinPulse strictly partitions device-level security secrets so they are **NEVER** synchronized to the cloud:
- Biometric hardware credentials and Android Keystore master keys never leave your physical device.
- PIN hashes, cryptographic salts, and keystore-encrypted secrets remain 100% on-device in sandboxed DataStore preferences.
- Ephemeral UI input caches, screenshot protection flags (`FLAG_SECURE`), and device notification alarms remain strictly local.


### C. Biometric Authentication & Device Security
FinPulse offers biometric unlock (Fingerprint and Face Unlock) and 4-6 digit PIN protection:
- **Zero Biometric Data Collection:** FinPulse **never** accesses, captures, scans, transmits, or stores raw biometric templates (such as facial contours or fingerprint scans). 
- All biometric verification is performed exclusively by the Android operating system through the native AndroidX `BiometricPrompt` framework and hardware-backed Android Keystore. The App only receives a cryptographic boolean success/failure signal (`AuthenticationResult`).
- Local PINs are salted with random cryptographic salts and hashed via SHA-256 before being stored in local preferences.

### D. System Notifications (Android 13+ / API 33+)
If you grant the `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` runtime permission, FinPulse issues local alerts for:
- Daily or weekly spending digests.
- Upcoming recurring subscription and bill due date reminders.
- Budget threshold warnings (e.g., reaching 70% or 100% of category allowance).

All notification evaluation and dispatch occurs **locally on your device** via Android WorkManager background tasks. No remote push notification services (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging / FCM) or external notification dispatch servers are utilized.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed by FinPulse is used exclusively to deliver the application's core functionality:
- Calculating net worth, available liquid balances, and double-entry consistency account transfers.
- Generating on-device spending visualizations (Canvas donut charts, cash flow bars, sparklines, and merchant leaderboards).
- Forecasting budget pacing and calculating suggested monthly contributions toward savings goals.
- Synchronizing records between Android devices linked to the same Google Account when cloud sync is explicitly enabled.
- Fetching current foreign currency exchange rates for multi-currency conversion using public reference APIs.

We **do not**:
- Use your financial records for profiling, automated credit scoring, or behavioral advertising.
- Sell or transmit financial logs to credit bureaus, banking institutions, or third-party marketing networks.
- Train artificial intelligence or machine learning models on your private ledgers.

## 5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We implement rigorous technical safeguards to secure your financial records:
- **Sandbox Isolation:** Local database files are stored in Android's private internal application directory (`/data/data/md.alexlab.finpulse/`), preventing access by other installed applications on unrooted devices.
- **In-Transit Encryption:** All communications with Google Cloud services use strict TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. Cleartext HTTP is explicitly prohibited at the application manifest level.
- **Server Security Rules:** Firestore database rules enforce that documents located at `/users/{userId}` are strictly readable and writable only by the authenticated user whose `request.auth.uid == userId`. No cross-account or public access is permitted.
- **Screenshot Protection:** When enabled in Security Settings, FinPulse applies `WindowManager.LayoutParams.FLAG_SECURE` to prevent OS screenshots and conceal application contents in the Android recent apps task switcher.
- **Android Backup Configuration:** Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer are governed by explicit `data_extraction_rules.xml` and `backup_rules.xml`. Temporary export caches and non-essential caches are explicitly excluded.

## 6. User Data Control, Rights & Deletion

You retain absolute ownership and control over your personal and financial information:

### A. Local Data Deletion
You can erase all local data at any time by:
1. Clearing application storage in Android System Settings: `Settings > Apps > FinPulse > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data`.
2. Uninstalling the FinPulse application from your device.

### B. In-App Cloud Data & Account Deletion
If you have activated Google Cloud Sync, you can permanently delete your remote cloud data and account directly within the application:
1. Open FinPulse and navigate to `More > Settings > Cloud Synchronization`.
2. Tap **"Delete Cloud Data & Account"**.
3. Confirm the deletion prompt.
4. The application immediately purges all Firestore documents located in `/users/{userId}` and signs out your session.

### C. Web-Based Account & Cloud Data Deletion Request
Pursuant to Google Play Store requirements, users who cannot access the mobile application may request the permanent deletion of their account and all synchronized cloud records via our public web deletion page:
- **Web Deletion URL:** `[INSERT PUBLIC DELETION URL, e.g., https://finpulse.app/delete-account]`
- **Alternative Email Request:** Send an email from your authenticated Google Account to `[INSERT PRIVACY EMAIL, e.g., privacy@finpulse.app]` with the subject line "Request Account and Data Deletion". Requests are verified and processed within 30 days.
