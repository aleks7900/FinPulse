Fin Pulse v1.0.0 — Welcome to Fin Pulse!

What's New:
• Complete personal finance tracker: Log expenses, income, and transfers seamlessly.
• Multi-Account & Wallet support: Manage checking, savings, credit cards, and cash.
• Category budgets with visual alerts and progress bars.

• Offline-First architecture: Works completely offline with zero latency.
• Optional Google Account Sync: Backup and sync all your financial records across Android devices via Google Sign-In.
• Biometric Lock (Fingerprint/PIN), Privacy Mode (balance masking), and screenshot protection.
