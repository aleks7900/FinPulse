Take complete control of your financial life with Fin Pulse — the smart, secure, and privacy-first personal finance tracker. Whether you are tracking daily coffee runs, organizing household budgets, planning savings goals, or monitoring multi-currency accounts, Fin Pulse makes personal money management fast, intuitive, and stress-free.

Unlike other finance apps that trap your data behind forced cloud walls or sell your financial habits to advertisers, Fin Pulse is built with an offline-first philosophy. Your data stays securely stored in an encrypted database on your device, and you choose when and how to synchronize across your Android devices using your private Google Account.
